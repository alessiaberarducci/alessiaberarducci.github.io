Research
interests

I am a PhD researcher in Computational Science at USI’s Statistical Computing Lab, with a background in statistics and mathematics. I study how to build models that remain reliable across environments.

My current work focuses on causal regularization under distribution shifts: obtaining stable, robust predictions even when we do not observe a reference setting. I also work on causal approaches to fairness in machine learning.
